I'm Thomas, a fire protection engineer based in Christchurch, New Zealand, with about five years in fire engineering consulting.

Before engineering, I was a firefighter in Taiwan. Seeing how buildings fail during a fire made me want to get them right at the design stage, which led me to postgraduate study in fire engineering at the University of Canterbury.

## What I do

I specialise in performance-based fire design:

- Fire design under the New Zealand **C/VM2** verification method
- Fire and smoke CFD modelling with **FDS / PyroSim**
- Evacuation analysis and occupant load assessment with **Pathfinder**
- Egress compliance and passive fire protection

## Fireline Perspective

Fireline Perspective (火線觀點) is my Mandarin-language content brand: the podcast *消防對不隊*, YouTube videos, and this site. Most articles here are in Traditional Chinese.
